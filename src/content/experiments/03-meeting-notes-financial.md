## Current status

This is a protocol, not a completed financial-services study. No practitioner outcome, time saving, or workflow recommendation is currently claimed.

## What the test asks

Can a local model turn approved, properly sanitized meeting material into a known CRM-note format while preserving facts and assigning actions to the correct person?

## Required test record

- At least 20 synthetic, public, or properly sanitized fixtures across routine and complex meetings.
- Exact machine, runtime, model artifact, quantization, context settings, and prompt template.
- Raw outputs and source-linked error annotations.
- Qualified review for invented figures, misattribution, omissions, dates, product references, and unsupported regulatory language.
- Edit-time and completion measurements using a fixed method.

## Pass conditions

Thresholds for factual accuracy, action-owner accuracy, critical errors, and time saved must be fixed before the run. Final records remain practitioner-owned and professionally reviewed.

## Out of scope

Investment recommendations, suitability decisions, tax advice, client-facing advice, or claims that local processing alone satisfies regulatory obligations.
