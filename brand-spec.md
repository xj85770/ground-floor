# Ground Floor — visual and interaction system

## Direction

Local AI instrument panel: bright, calm, exact, and inspectable. It should feel like a tool used beside a professional workpaper—not an AI laboratory, editorial manifesto, crypto product, or startup pitch deck.

## Identity

- Wordmark: `Ground Floor` as text. No invented symbol.
- Signature object: a real task → Mac → cautious setup reading in the first viewport.
- Evidence labels are plain language at the claim: `Measured`, `Estimate`, `Third-party`, or `Protocol—not yet tested`.
- No synthetic people, fake testimonials, fake terminals, abstract AI art, or decorative dashboards.
- One purposeful Mac-like device may appear in the opening interaction when it acts as the transition into the real setup flow: closed device → brief opening motion → live Ground Floor interface. It must be skippable, respect reduced-motion preferences, and never delay access to the first useful question.
- The current opening target is the 16-inch MacBook Pro with M5 Max in Space Black. Procedural geometry must follow Apple’s published 35.57 × 24.81 × 1.68 cm chassis, 3456×2234 display ratio, 78-key layout, notch, speaker fields, Force Touch trackpad, and published port families. Describe it as dimensionally anchored, never CAD-exact.
- Decorative hardware silhouettes, repeated device mockups, gratuitous 3D spectacle, and hardware used only as background ornament remain prohibited.

## Palette

- Canvas: `#F2F4F3`
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Ink: `#111820`
- Body: `#33404A`
- Muted: `#63707A`
- Rule: `#D5DCE0`
- Action/focus: `#075EA8`
- Action hover: `#084B83`
- Protocol: `#426B8A`
- Correction: `#A23B2A`
- Success: `#1F6B45`

Forbidden: purple, gradients, glow, glass, auroras, neon, decorative status colors, shadows on ordinary content, pill-heavy UI.

### Optional dark theme

- The default remains the bright instrument system above.
- A user-selected dark theme may use black-violet canvas `#08070F`, surface `#11101A`, ink `#F7F5FF`, body `#C7C2D7`, muted `#938CA7`, and rule `#29243B`.
- Dark-theme action range: electric blue `#2F7BFF` → violet `#8B5CF6`. The gradient pulse is reserved for the primary action and focus state; it must not color evidence labels or ordinary content.
- Theme choice persists locally. Motion stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Typography

- Interface, headings, body: self-hosted IBM Plex Sans.
- Model identifiers and evidence metadata only: self-hosted IBM Plex Mono.
- H1: 56/58 desktop, 40/42 mobile, 600, maximum 18 characters per visual line where practical.
- Body: 16/26; primary lede 18/29. No essential text below 15px.
- Literal question headings outrank slogans.

## Layout

- Maximum width: 1160px. Desktop padding: 40px; mobile: 20px.
- 8px spacing system. Sections: 72–88px desktop, 48–56px mobile.
- Rectangular controls, 0–4px radius, 1px rules, 48px primary controls.
- Prefer rows, forms, tables, and paired comparisons over card grids.
- One primary action per viewport. No layout-shifting hover.
- The cinematic entrance plays automatically for a first-time visitor, then gets out of the way. Returning visits reveal the live setup interface immediately, with a replay control available outside the primary task flow. A direct `?intro=1` preview may force the animation for review.

## Voice and sequence

Customer job → what Ground Floor does → visible answer → material limits → evidence → human invitation.

Use concrete customer language. Explain the mechanism only after the outcome. State inconvenient facts beside the offer, not in distant disclaimers.
